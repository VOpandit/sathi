Sathi helps neighbors share surplus food (such as 2 plates of rice left over from an event), study books, clothes, elderly care, and urgent help with people nearby.

How Sathi Works with WhatsApp

When a Helper posts surplus food or a Needy user claims an item, Sathi alerts nearby members on WhatsApp and generates a 4-digit Pickup PIN so handovers stay safe and quick.

Five Sathi Community Pillars

🍱 FoodShare

Extra food share karne — leftover event food & fresh meals.

📚 EduBridge

Books ani study material share karne — textbooks, notes & kits.

👕 NeedNotWaste

Clothes ani useful items donate karne — clean clothes & essentials.

👵 CareConnect

Elderly lokanna basic assistance — errands, medicine & support.

🤝 HelpHub

General help requests — medicines, bill support & urgent aid.
